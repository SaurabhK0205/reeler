Reeler

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Overview

Reeler, operated by SAURABH'S ("we", "our", or "the Service"), is a utility that enables users to save Instagram Reels and Posts sent via Direct Messages into a personal watch-later vault and receive scheduled reminder notifications. We respect your privacy and are committed to processing only the minimal data required to deliver the core service.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with Reeler on Instagram or via our web application, we process the following information:

  • Instagram Scoped User ID (IGSID): A pseudonymous identifier assigned by Meta that allows our bot to send direct messages and schedule reminders for you.
  • Message Content and Reel URLs: The links, media identifiers, and text of the Reels you share directly with the bot.
  • Reminder Settings: Timing preferences (such as 1 hour, tonight, tomorrow, or custom intervals) that you request for notifications.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, contact books, or browsing history outside of the links you explicitly send to the service.

3. How We Use Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Scheduling and dispatching reminder notifications via the Instagram Messenger API.
  • Populating your personal web-accessible reel archive and watch list.
  • Verifying ownership when accessing your personal reel vault via one-time passcodes or secure keys.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data exchanges are limited to:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: Interaction with the official Instagram Graph and Messenger APIs to receive incoming webhook events and transmit response messages.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Secure hosting and database storage services (Render and Supabase) utilizing industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Saved Reels and reminder records are retained until you mark them as completed or request their deletion. You have full control over your data:

  • Send delete my data directly to the bot on Instagram to immediately and permanently delete all your saved reels and user records.
  • Send stop all or clear all to dismiss active reminder notifications and mark pending reels as watched without deleting your saved archive.
  • Refer to our Data Deletion Instructions for detailed instructions on automated and manual data purge requests.
