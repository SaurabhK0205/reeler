User Data Deletion Instructions

In compliance with Meta Platform Policy • Last updated: November 15, 2026

Data Privacy & Control

Reeler, operated by SAURABH'S, values your data autonomy. You can request the permanent deletion of your user account, stored Reels, reminder history, and all associated metadata at any time. We offer two simple ways to initiate data deletion.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

Step 1: Open Instagram Direct Message

Navigate to your Instagram direct messages and open your chat conversation with the Reeler bot.

Step 2: Send Permanent Deletion Command

Send the text message delete my data or delete account to the bot.

Step 3: Immediate Purge

The bot immediately purges all your saved reels, reminder queue entries, and user records from the database and confirms permanent deletion in the chat.

Note: To only dismiss active reminders without erasing your saved library, you can alternatively send stop all or clear all.

Method 2: Manual Request via Support

If you no longer have access to your Instagram account or prefer manual verification:

  1. Send an email to saurabhk0205@gmail.com with the subject line Reeler Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your Instagram username (handle) or your Instagram Scoped User ID.
  3. Our support team will verify your identifier and permanently erase all associated database records within 48 hours.
  4. A confirmation email will be sent back upon completion with confirmation of record eradication.

What Gets Deleted

Upon receiving a deletion request, the following records are permanently removed from our storage systems:

  • Your Instagram Scoped User ID (IGSID) mapping.
  • All saved Reel URLs, titles, and cached metadata associated with your account.
  • All pending and completed reminder records.
  • All active vault authentication keys and one-time passcodes.
